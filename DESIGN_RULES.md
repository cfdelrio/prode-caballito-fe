# ProdeCaballito - Design Rules

## Brand Identity

ProdeCaballito is not a casino product.
It is a football prediction experience inspired by:

* ESPN
* TyC Sports
* FIFA World Cup broadcasts
* Champions League graphics
* Sports competition culture

The experience must feel:

* competitive
* energetic
* football-oriented
* modern
* immersive
* social

NOT:

* generic SaaS
* corporate dashboard
* crypto casino
* betting scam aesthetic

---

# Visual Direction

## Core Feeling

The UI should feel like:

* a live football event
* a sports TV package
* a competitive tournament
* a modern fantasy football experience

Every screen should create:

* anticipation
* adrenaline
* movement
* competition

---

# Colors

## Primary Palette

* Deep black backgrounds
* Dark graphite
* Stadium shadows
* Electric green accents
* White typography
* Occasional Argentina sky blue accents

## Avoid

* flat gray interfaces
* overly colorful UI
* random gradients
* soft startup palettes

---

# Typography

## Headlines

Large.
Aggressive.
Sports broadcast style.

Examples:

* uppercase
* bold
* compact spacing
* impactful hierarchy

Headlines must feel:

* dramatic
* energetic
* urgent

---

# UI Components

## Buttons

CTA buttons must:

* stand out immediately
* feel clickable
* feel premium
* use strong contrast

Preferred:

* glowing effects
* subtle gradients
* motion on hover
* bold labels

Examples:

* JUGAR
* SUMARME
* VER RANKING
* ENTRAR AL PRODE

---

# Cards

Cards should:

* feel dynamic
* use depth/shadow
* never look flat
* include visual hierarchy

Ranking cards should feel:

* competitive
* alive
* exciting

---

# Motion

Animations should:

* feel smooth
* feel premium
* feel sporty

Preferred:

* subtle camera movement
* scoreboard transitions
* glowing hover effects
* animated rankings
* confetti/light streaks

Avoid:

* excessive animation
* slow animations
* childish motion

---

# Empty States

Empty states should NEVER feel dead.

Always include:

* football energy
* CTA
* motion
* excitement

Examples:

* “Todavía no hay partidos cargados”
* “Preparando la próxima fecha”
* “El ranking todavía está vacío”

Always visually support empty states.

---

# Mobile Experience

Mobile is PRIORITY.

Design for:

* vertical consumption
* quick reading
* thumb interaction
* sports app behavior

The app should feel:

* native
* fast
* immersive

---

# Desktop Experience

Desktop should feel:

* cinematic
* immersive
* ESPN-like
* premium sports platform

Use:

* stadium lighting
* dramatic spacing
* layered depth

---

# Images

Preferred imagery:

* real stadiums
* football crowds
* dramatic lighting
* fans
* football atmosphere
* match tension

Avoid:

* fake stock business people
* generic startup illustrations
* corporate office visuals

---

# Tone of Voice

Language must feel:

* Argentine
* football-oriented
* exciting
* natural
* competitive

Examples:

* “¿Te la bancás?”
* “Demostrá cuánto sabés”
* “Ya podés jugar”
* “Ranking en vivo”
* “Se viene la próxima fecha”

Avoid:

* robotic marketing copy
* corporate wording
* English startup jargon

---

# UX Rules

Every screen must answer:

1. What is happening?
2. Why should I care?
3. What should I do next?

Always prioritize:

* clarity
* conversion
* excitement
* speed

---

# Technical UI Rules

* Keep interfaces responsive
* Respect existing design system
* Avoid visual clutter
* Prefer reusable UI patterns
* Keep accessibility acceptable
* Optimize rendering performance
* Avoid unnecessary complexity

---

# Final Principle

The experience should feel like:
“the World Cup meets modern digital product design.”

Not a spreadsheet.
Not a casino.
Not a generic SaaS.

A football event people want to be part of.
